__TITLE__ streets

Edit: drag a point to move it · click a line to select it, Delete removes it · right-click a point to remove it · click on a line while holding Shift to add a point there. Draw: click to place points (starting or ending on an existing point or line joins them) · Enter or double-click finishes · Esc cancels. Magenta rings are crossings without a shared point: the lines don't join there (a bridge); click one in Edit mode to join them. A yellow ring is a place almost as near a second street (dashed) as its nearest, where the two only meet a long way round: draw a spoke from the street you'd really reach it by. Orange dots are Mapzeroth's entrances, lifts, flight masters, docks and portals; blue ones its other places, grey ones places whose walks don't use the streets (Undercity's ruins and lift tops); all for reference, not editable.
