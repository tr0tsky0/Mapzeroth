Written by gen_borders.py each run; not read by the addon.

Zone-to-zone walking crossings (Data/Forever/Borders.lua): a pair of nodes, one just inside each zone, joined
by a walk edge. A crossing with no `cost` has real travel inside it (a tunnel); the engine works the cost out
from the distance between its two ends. Coordinates were read off the in-game world map, so they are rough:
refine by walking a crossing and capturing `/mzdump here`.

Checked and NOT walkable (left out on purpose):
  Elwynn Forest <-> Duskwood: Technically you can just swim across the southern river into Duskwood, but there's no "road" crossing
  Redridge Mountains <-> Burning Steppes: Doesn't Exist...it's part of Riverglades now
  Dun Morogh <-> Wetlands: Does Not Exist
